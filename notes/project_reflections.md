# Project Reflections

#### About Dataset <br>
Dataset: The dataset, obtained from a free source, was in good condition from the start, requiring only minor adjustments to provide context. Furthermore, the data had already undergone a transformation process—a fact that, in my view, presents both advantages and disadvantages. On the positive side, it offers greater flexibility and shortens the time required for analysis; on the negative side, some of the transformations performed appear unnecessary for the purposes of this analysis.

#### About Business Context<br>
- Since there was no brief and this was a personal project, the entire business context is framed from my own perspective; therefore, it may not accurately represent the realities of the café business in the real world.
