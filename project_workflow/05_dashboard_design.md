# Dashboard Design

## Design Dashboard Process

### 1. Decision <br>
The dashboard will help the owner make decisions on which products to promote in order to increase total sales.

### 2. Audience <br>
- User: Owner (Direct business owner) <br>
- Level of detail: From a high-level, exploratory overview to granular, specific details

### 3. Signal <br>
- Metric: Total sales, AOV, Total Orders
- Dimensions: Date, Time, Weekday, coffee_name, cash_type

### 4. Hierarchy <br>
Top-side.
| KPI | KPI | KPI| Title |
| --- | --- | ---| --- |
| Slicer | Slicer | Slicer | Slicer |

Bottom-side.
| Sales vs AOV trend (money - Time) | Sales by Product (money - coffee_name) | Sales Table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales by Day | Sales by Hour | Payment Method|
